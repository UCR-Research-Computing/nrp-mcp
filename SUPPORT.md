# Getting help

- **Using nrp-mcp:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/UCR-Research-Computing/nrp-mcp/issues)
  (question or bug).
- **UCR researchers:** research-computing@ucr.edu, and the
  [Nautilus guides](https://ucr-research-computing.github.io/kb/kb023-nautilus-researcher-guide/).
- **The Nautilus cluster itself** (accounts, namespaces, quotas, outages): the NRP's support
  channels at https://nrp.ai/contact.
